The pattern is engraved 0.8 mm into the 1.6 mm side walls and runs over the whole face, so it makes the part lighter, not heavier. The roof carries straight lines across it, like the courses of a shingled roof, engraved 0.5 mm. In this viewer the engraved areas are drawn darker so they can be seen.

The doorbell's front details (glass, lens, button) are drawn approximately. The body colour is the real white.
